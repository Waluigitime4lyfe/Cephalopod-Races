## Brief Description

Cephalopods are an eldritch race of octopus and squids that need oxygenated water to survive. They are escape artists that can make a cloud of ink, and run very slightly faster without shoes on. 

## Background

Squid faced aliens have been a staple in sci fi and fantasy for a long time. While I've never played space station 13, some wiki's talk about a humanoid with squid genetics being playable. This along with the squiddy cosmetic in 14 show their inclusion wouldn't be far fetched. Obsessed with cephalopods since childhood, I wholeheartedly believe the most intelligent invertebrates in real life should get some representation.

### Description

 Breathing
- The breathing mechanic is removed in favor of their thirst mechanic's changes. 
- When critical over dehydration, they need to be treated for oxygen deprivation.
- Toxic atmosphere will still be a threat, as they will still get it on their skin.
  - If not, more of a threat to them, as they cannot use internals. To combat this, they'll need a suit and helmet. (Any protective suit or space suit that would normally keep bad atmosphere out will work)
- Standing in a water tile will automatically start hydrating you.

 Stats
- They get thirstier 60% faster than other races.
- They succumb to low and high pressure sickness 50% faster.
- 10% faster when not wearing shoes.

 Ink Ability
- Creates a cloud on the same floor tile as the player.
- The cloud spreads out to all connected tiles to the first one (like a cleanade) but stops spreading immediately after.
- The 9 cloud tiles will linger for 8 seconds.
- After disappearing, it will leave behind 9 small puddles of ink. (smallest puddle size) 
  - not slippery 
  - Can be collected like other liquids
   - When drank, ink "tastes fishy"
   - When sprayed with a spray gun or spilled on someone, causes complete blindness for 5 seconds. 
- When in the cloud of ink, players will not be able to see anything and will be slowed down just like spider webs. Cephalopods are immune to being slowed down by it.
 
Visual Design
- Images attached.
- Cephalopods will look like an octopus or squid, standing upright on its long arms and tentacles. When naked, their body is just 8 arms that trail from their "head" to the floor, with two acting as the conventional arms. 
- Customization options will be Head shape (Round or conelike), Head addon (Fins, or even shells if fun enough), Face tentacles that will act as the facial hair options, and patterns you can choose. Some patterns are in the example images attached.
- Color Customization options will be limited to their skin color, their suction cup skin color, their eyes, the color of the pattern you select, and the color of whatever head addons you pick.
- When gibbed, organs will be gills, hearts, brains, ink sac, and stomach. Some provided in one of the attached images.

 Audio Design
- Walking without shoes will make a multitude of fast suction cup noises
- Screaming will play the sound of spraying water
- An option in the quick action radial (Like how the diona can chirp and the Arachnid can click) is to squelch, this plays the suction cup noise.

 Language
- Chat messages when they speak imply they are using their skins colors to communicate. (Nanotransen's future technology lets these signals travel on even audio devices, so no need to worry if you need to use a radio.) 
- When they are technically yelling, they'll be using their siphon instead.
- Messages will begin with words like "mimics," "flashes," "strobes," "shifts," to show this
- Yelled Messages will begin with words like "sputters," "spits," "sprays," "squelches,"

 Naming conventions
- Lovecraftian inspired names. Some with aquatic themes.
- Can have multiple names. 
 - Cthuloss, Ykharus Drejj, Maltomaar Khrakon, BasPrawnn, Niptuun, Aazathoth, GharrTunah (As examples)

### Guidebook

 (Image of base Cephalopod here)
They cannot wear anything in the mask slot, and do not need internals. When not wearing shoes, they make suction cup noises when they step but become 10% faster. They get thirstier 60% faster than other races. They don't breathe like others and need to drink water instead. (Thanks to Nanotransen, all water sources are safe and oxygenated!) Liquids other than water count as food to them. Adding salt to water will make it more hydrating for them. They succumb to low and high pressure sickness 50% faster. Their blue blood cannot be metabolized with iron, as it is based on copper instead.

Ink defense
 (Image of ink cloud, ink puddle, and metamorphic glass of ink here in that order)
Cephalopods can spurt a cloud of ink that spreads from them outwards, covering 9 tiles including the one they were standing on. People in the ink will be slowed down and will not see anything until it fades away, leaving 9 small puddles of ink on the ground. All Cephalopods are immune to the slow effect, but not the visibility change. When collected, ink can be drank safely or thrown at someone to blind them for 5 seconds.
